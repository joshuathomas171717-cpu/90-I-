01 · Form, without fake recency

Minutes-weighted, competition-adjusted output, shrunk toward 50 for sparse samples. Dated matches use a 35-day half-life; season totals explicitly say recency unavailable. A goalkeeper needs rating data, not goals. Scores below are descriptive indices, not win probabilities.

02 · What a team loses, with a replacement counted

Tier 1: position-weighted share of the club's goals. A replacement retains 70% by assumption; the range uses 50–85% retained contribution. These are scenario assumptions, not confidence intervals. Defensive and goalkeeper role shares are priors.

Tier 2: output with/without a player, adjusted for opponent and home advantage, using only completed prior matches. It needs at least 10 appearances and 5 explicit zero-minute records. Missing records are unknown, not injuries. The fitted effect is an association, not a causal injury estimate—rotation and selection can explain it.

03 · Rest and travel, only when actually known

Exact kickoff intervals and explicitly supplied travel distances replace the candidate's crude European-membership proxy. An international window is not proof someone played; only named appearances count. Away travel without a distance stays unknown. Today the layer has 0 exact next kickoffs; fixture windows are not converted into invented match dates. The live model's old proxy remains unchanged until the new head is validated.

How to reproduce, and what opens the gate

python3 player_gate.py builds the report; python3 player_context.py builds the indices; python3 run_all.py rebuilds the site.

Exact historical dates, appearances and separately timestamped pre-match captures are required. The first 120 fixtures warm up the features; later calls see only prior completed matches. No season-end totals or target lineups enter the features. Policy constants are fixed before evaluation.

Requirements: ≥200 test matches, ≥80% pre-match coverage, RPS gain ≥0.002, improved hit rate, and the upper endpoint of a paired seven-day-block RPS-delta interval below zero. The actual production head then needs validation too.

A free-provider backfill is optional and resumable; it never starts automatically in CI. Manual history/calendar drops work without a key. See the full model card and input schema.

Now visible: source-labelled squad panels, named-player What-If input ranges and snapshot-derived matchweek digests. Legacy locks without availability stay visibly unrecorded; later team news is never backfilled.
